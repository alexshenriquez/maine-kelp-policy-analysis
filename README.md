# Maine Kelp Aquaculture Policy Analysis
Independent research project examining the environmental,
geographic, economic, and regulatory factors that may
influence sustainable seaweed aquaculture development in Maine.
## Status
Research design and data collection — October 2026
## Research Question
What geographic, environmental, economic, and regulatory
factors should Maine consider when identifying areas
potentially suitable for sustainable seaweed aquaculture?
