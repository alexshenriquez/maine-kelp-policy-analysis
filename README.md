# Maine Kelp Aquaculture Policy Analysis

## Overview
This independent research project examines the potential for sustainable seaweed aquaculture development in Maine.
The project evaluates environmental, geographic, economic, infrastructure, regulatory, and social factors that may influence where seaweed aquaculture is viable and what conditions may be necessary for responsible expansion.

## Research Questions
### Primary Research Question
Where in Maine is seaweed aquaculture potentially viable, and what environmental, economic, social, infrastructure, and regulatory factors determine that viability?

### Secondary Research Question
What policy or regulatory changes could enable sustainable seaweed aquaculture development in areas with potential for viability, while protecting marine ecosystems and addressing competing coastal uses?

## Research Objectives
1. Identify areas of Maine with characteristics potentially favorable to sustainable seaweed aquaculture.
2. Identify environmental, geographic, economic, infrastructure, regulatory, and social constraints on development.
3. Assess potential environmental, economic, and community benefits associated with expanded seaweed aquaculture.
4. Assess potential environmental, economic, and social tradeoffs associated with expansion.
5. Develop evidence-based policy options for Maine policymakers, agencies, municipalities, and industry stakeholders.

## Key Evidence Categories
* Environmental suitability
* Geographic suitability
* Economic viability
* Infrastructure
* Regulatory conditions
* Social and community considerations

## Working Hypothesis
Maine has areas with meaningful potential for sustainable seaweed aquaculture, but expansion is constrained by a combination of environmental requirements, competing coastal uses, regulatory processes, infrastructure limitations, and market conditions rather than by a single regulatory barrier.

This hypothesis will be evaluated against available evidence and may be revised as the research progresses.

## Methodology
The project will combine:
* Policy and regulatory analysis
* Literature review
* Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
* Public environmental and economic datasets
* Data analysis using SQL and Python
* Spatial analysis
* Data visualization
* Stakeholder and competing-use analysis

## Policy Question
Do Maine's existing policy and regulatory frameworks appropriately balance the potential environmental, economic, and community benefits of seaweed aquaculture with legitimate environmental protections and competing coastal uses?
