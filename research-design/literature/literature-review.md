# Literature Review

## Purpose

This literature review will examine existing research, policy documents, government reports, and other credible sources related to seaweed aquaculture in Maine and comparable regions.

The review will identify what is already known about the environmental, geographic, economic, regulatory, infrastructure, and social factors that influence the viability and responsible expansion of seaweed aquaculture.

The literature review will also identify gaps in existing research that this project can address through original data analysis and policy research.

## 8. Research Gaps

The literature review will identify areas where existing research is limited or where additional analysis could improve policy decisions.

Potential research gaps include:

* Geographic analysis of seaweed aquaculture suitability in Maine
* Integration of environmental and economic suitability
* Analysis of infrastructure constraints
* Spatial analysis of competing coastal uses
* Comparison of Maine's regulatory framework with other jurisdictions
* Analysis of policy barriers to responsible industry expansion
* Distribution of potential economic benefits and costs
* Identification of areas where targeted policy intervention may have the greatest impact

These research gaps will be revised as the literature review progresses.
