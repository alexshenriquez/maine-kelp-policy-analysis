# Literature Review

## Purpose

This literature review will examine existing research on seaweed aquaculture, with particular attention to environmental suitability, economic viability, ecological impacts, coastal policy, regulation, and community considerations in Maine and comparable regions.

## Research Themes

### 1. Environmental Suitability

Questions to investigate:

* What environmental conditions influence kelp growth?
* What geographic and oceanographic conditions are associated with successful cultivation?
* What environmental risks or ecological impacts have been identified?

### 2. Economic Viability

Questions to investigate:

* What are the costs of seaweed aquaculture?
* What markets exist for cultivated seaweed?
* What factors influence profitability?
* What infrastructure is required?
* What economic benefits have been documented?

### 3. Environmental Benefits

Questions to investigate:

* What evidence exists regarding carbon sequestration?
* What evidence exists regarding nutrient removal?
* What evidence exists regarding habitat effects?
* What are the limitations of existing research?

### 4. Environmental Risks and Tradeoffs

Questions to investigate:

* What ecological risks have been identified?
* Could cultivation affect existing marine ecosystems?
* What monitoring approaches have been proposed?

### 5. Regulatory and Policy Frameworks

Questions to investigate:

* How is seaweed aquaculture regulated in Maine?
* What federal regulations apply?
* How do other jurisdictions regulate seaweed aquaculture?
* What regulatory challenges have researchers identified?

### 6. Coastal Communities and Competing Uses

Questions to investigate:

* How does seaweed aquaculture interact with commercial fishing?
* What community concerns have been documented?
* What conflicts may occur with other marine uses?
* How are benefits and costs distributed?
